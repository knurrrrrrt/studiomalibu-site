Three steps

From a thought to text, without leaving the app you are in.

01

Hold the hotkey

Press and hold in any app. An indicator shows that it is listening. Double-tap instead for hands-free recording.

02

Speak

Transcription runs with the engine you chose. Dictionary entries, snippets and spoken punctuation are applied.

03

Text appears

The result is inserted by paste (Cmd+V) at your cursor. Your previous clipboard is restored after a time you set.

What's inside

What the current version (0.10.6) actually does.

Dictate

Three dictation modes

Raw transcript as spoken, a Claude rewrite that cleans up the text, or a clipboard rewrite that reworks what you have copied. Pick the mode that fits the moment.

Four speech engines

Parakeet (v3 and Parakeet Ultra), WhisperKit, Nemotron and Apple SpeechAnalyzer on macOS 26 and later. Parakeet Ultra lowers the German word error rate from 5.6 to 4.6 percent; it is about a 630 MB download and falls back to v3.

Meeting mode (beta)

Opt-in. Records microphone and system audio as separate streams, transcribes on your Mac, and summarizes via Claude or an offline Basic summary. Markers, templates, a glossary and Markdown export with an Obsidian-style header.

Per-app profiles

Different behavior for different apps, set once.

Dictionary and snippets

A personal dictionary, snippets and spoken punctuation.

Hands-free

Double-tap the hotkey to keep recording without holding it.

Clipboard, handled

Text goes in by paste. Timed clipboard retention and restore bring back what you had copied, so dictating does not eat your clipboard.

Indicators and history

Show the recording state as a Notch, Overlay or Minimal indicator, and look back at past dictations in the history.

Good to know

What you need, and what it does not do yet.

Requirements

  • macOS 15.0 or later; the Apple Speech engine needs macOS 26 or later
  • Audio stays on your Mac; rewrite and summaries need your own Claude API key
  • Free trial, then a one-time license

Not there yet

  • Meeting mode is beta and off until you opt in
  • Double-tap timing and the marker shortcut (Ctrl+Opt+M) are still being tested in daily use
  • Nemotron's German is untested
