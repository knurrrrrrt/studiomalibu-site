Three steps

From a thought to text, without leaving the app you are in.

01

Hold the hotkey

Press and hold in any app. Caps Lock and fn work as hotkeys. An indicator shows that it is listening. Double-tap instead for hands-free recording (opt-in).

02

Speak

Transcription runs with the engine you chose. Dictionary entries, snippets and spoken punctuation are applied.

03

Text appears

The result is inserted by paste (Cmd+V) at your cursor. Your previous clipboard is restored after a time you set.

What's inside

What the current version (0.10.6) actually does.

Dictate

Three dictation modes

Raw transcript as spoken, a rewrite that cleans up the text (Claude in the cloud, or local with Apple Intelligence), or a clipboard rewrite that reworks what you have copied. Pick the mode that fits the moment.

Four speech engines

Parakeet (v3 and Parakeet Ultra), WhisperKit, Nemotron (English only) and Apple SpeechAnalyzer on macOS 26 and later. In our own FLEURS benchmark (German only), Parakeet Ultra lowers the word error rate from 5.6 to 4.6 percent; it is about a 600 MB download and falls back to v3.

Meeting mode (beta)

Opt-in. Records microphone and system audio as separate streams, transcribes on your Mac, and summarizes via Claude or an offline Basic summary. Markers, templates and a glossary. Export as Markdown with “Save as .md”, or to a folder (opt-in) with an Obsidian-style header.

Per-app profiles

Different rewrite behavior for different apps, set once. Profiles apply to the rewrite modes, not to Raw.

Dictionary and snippets

A personal dictionary, snippets, trigger words and spoken punctuation.

Hands-free (opt-in)

Off by default. Once enabled, double-tap the hotkey to keep recording without holding it.

Clipboard, handled

Text goes in by paste. Timed clipboard retention and restore bring back what you had copied, so dictating does not eat your clipboard.

Indicators and history (opt-in)

Show the recording state as a Notch, Overlay or Minimal indicator. The history of past dictations is off by default and opt-in.

Good to know

What you need, and what it does not do yet.

Requirements

  • macOS 15.0 or later; the Apple Speech engine and the local rewrite (Apple Intelligence) need macOS 26 or later
  • Audio stays on your Mac. The “Claude (Cloud)” rewrite and Claude meeting summaries need your own Claude API key; the local rewrite (Apple Intelligence) needs none
  • 30-day free trial; an email address is required at first launch
  • Needs Microphone and Accessibility permission
  • Needs Microphone and Accessibility permission

Not there yet

  • Meeting mode is beta and off until you opt in
  • Double-tap timing and the marker shortcut (Ctrl+Opt+M) are still being tested in daily use
  • Nemotron: English only
  • Meeting mode labels speakers only as “You” and “Others”, no speaker names
  • The Claude meeting summary sends the transcript text to Anthropic
  • In password fields nothing is pasted automatically; the text stays on the clipboard
  • Live Transcript Preview is experimental
