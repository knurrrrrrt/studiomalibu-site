Three steps

From a finished cut to a conformed Resolve timeline.

01

Export from Final Cut Pro

Export the finished timeline as FCPXML. That file is what Malibu Conform works from.

02

Conform reads and resolves

Multicam angles, sync clips and compounds are resolved, speed ramps are snapped, and unsupported filters are flagged.

03

Timeline in Resolve Studio

A bundled driver applies the transforms in DaVinci Resolve Studio and reads the values back. A Conform Report as PDF documents the result.

What's inside

What the current beta (0.1.10) does.

Spatial

Spatial conform, baked in

Position, scale and crop are conformed into the Resolve timeline, including the pixel aspect ratio, so the framing you set in Final Cut Pro is the framing you get.

Multicam, sync clips, compounds

Multicam angles are resolved, sync clips are unwrapped and compound clips are inlined before the timeline is built.

Resolve Studio driver

A bundled driver (Python 3.11) applies the transforms in Resolve Studio and reads the values back. Resolve can be started automatically.

Speed ramps

Speed-ramp times are snapped to NTSC-rational frame rates.

Burn-in bars

Bars are derived automatically from the proxy and original size.

Burn-in rebase

Transforms are rebased for burn-ins, with Pan/Tilt unit handling.

Unsupported filters, flagged

Final Cut Pro filters that are not supported are removed and listed as red markers, so nothing disappears silently.

Conform Report as PDF

Every conform produces a report as PDF that you can check and hand on.

Good to know

What you need, and what it does not do yet.

Requirements

  • macOS 14.0 or later
  • DaVinci Resolve Studio is required
  • Final Cut Pro timelines exported as FCPXML
  • Currently in beta with testers (v0.1.10), not released yet

Not there yet

  • Video only; audio conform is deferred
  • Only track 1 is addressed; transforms on connected clips are not applied
  • Keyframed clips get a warning only
  • Verified against one Resolve version so far (21.1.0.17)
  • Pan/Tilt is measured only for Crop and Fit

In the works
