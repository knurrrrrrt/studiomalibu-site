Four steps

From script and clips to scenes in Final Cut Pro.

01

Import script and clips

Load the screenplay PDF and the camera clips from an exported .fcpxml or .fcpxmld.

02

Transcribe

Each clip is transcribed individually, locally on your Mac.

03

Match and check

Every transcript is matched against the screenplay. Scene Check shows you where it needs a second look.

04

Export to Final Cut Pro

Keywords go into Final Cut Pro via FCPXML import (Send to FCP).

What's inside

What the current version (1.2.85) actually does.

Match

Per-clip transcription, matched to the script

Each camera clip is transcribed on its own with Parakeet v3 in 25 European languages. The result is matched against the screenplay.

Scene Check and scene aliases

Review how every clip landed. Use "Also Use for Clips of Scene…" when scenes share a setup, and use Add Scene Manually to add scenes yourself.

Keywords

Results go into Final Cut Pro via FCPXML import as Keywords. Export presets and keyword handles are included.

Block Editor

A Block Editor sits next to the Classic List, so you can work the way you prefer. A mini timeline snaps to sentence starts.

Action Cut

Drag out the Action Cut range and reject everything outside it.

Your shortcuts

Freely rebindable shortcuts, a ripple-trim shortcut, Magnetic Ripple, and next/previous clip that skips collapsed multicam rows.

Your script stays local

Script and dialog never leave the Mac. The optional local LLM verifier uses Ollama on localhost; it is off by default and only runs when Ollama is running with a model.

Sessions and diagnostics

Save and reopen your work as sessions. When something looks off, export diagnostics to send along with your feedback.

Good to know

What you need, and what it does not do yet.

Requirements

  • macOS 15.0 or later on Apple Silicon (arm64), Final Cut Pro
  • Transcription and matching run locally and script and dialog stay on your Mac; the license/trial check and the update check connect online
  • Free trial; an email address is required at first launch. The in-app feedback button only sends when you press it

Not there yet

  • Screenplay import is PDF only (no Fountain or FDX)
  • Final Cut Pro only (no Premiere or Resolve)
  • The newest parts of the interface are still being checked in daily use
  • The first transcription needs internet to download the speech model
