What's inside

What the current version (2.4.0) actually does.

Clipboard

History that respects Final Cut Pro

MalibuClip keeps what you copy in the menu bar, including Final Cut Pro clipboard content with its format intact. The history is encrypted at rest with AES-GCM.

Screenshots

Capture, annotate, and polish with Beautify. Scrolling capture and OCR are built in.

Screen recording

Record your screen with system audio, microphone and camera, then refine it in the recording editor with its zoom timeline.

Auto-Zoom

Zoom follows your cursor during the recording, and you adjust it afterwards on the timeline.

Look and feel

Set a recording background (colour, gradient or image), padding, corners and shadow, plus a click highlight.

GIF export

Export a recording as a GIF, up to 72 seconds. Beta.

Final Cut Pro export

Export an FCPXML with zoom keyframes, the camera as picture-in-picture, and optional captions.

FCP Buffer

10 to 20 numbered clipboard slots, each with its own hotkey and a malibuclip://buffer/... URL you can call from elsewhere.

Good to know

What you need, and what it does not do yet.

Requirements

  • macOS 26.0 or later
  • Apple Silicon Mac
  • Free trial, then a one-time license

Not there yet

  • GIF export and Studio Mode are still in beta
  • Studio Mode records without the cursor and draws it smoothed at export. The cursor is always an arrow there, and it is absent in the Final Cut Pro export
  • FCP Buffer slots are stored unencrypted
  • The newest recording features are checked with pixel tests and are still being proven in daily use
