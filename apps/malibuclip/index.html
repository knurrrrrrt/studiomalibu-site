What's inside

What the current version (2.4.0) actually does.

Clipboard

History that respects Final Cut Pro

MalibuClip keeps what you copy in the menu bar, including Final Cut Pro clipboard content with its format intact. Clipboard contents are encrypted at rest. A blocklist ignores password managers, text clips are editable, and Space opens a Quick Look preview. The optional Notch Shelf is off by default and replaces the ⌘⇧V panel when switched on.

Screenshots

Capture, annotate, crop and polish with Beautify. Hotkeys cover a window, the full screen and the last area. Capture Text (OCR) is one hotkey away, and scrolling capture is built in. MalibuClip can stand in for the ⇧⌘3/4/5 shortcuts, and Save to folder is available.

Screen recording (Beta)

Record your screen with system audio, microphone and camera, then refine it in the recording editor with its zoom timeline.

Auto-Zoom

Auto-Zoom is calculated after the recording, driven by your clicks and cursor data. You adjust it on the timeline. Older recordings without cursor data are not supported.

Look and feel

Set a recording background (colour, gradient or image), padding, corners and shadow, plus a click highlight.

GIF export

Export a recording as a GIF: about 30 seconds at the default 12 fps. Longer recordings lower the frame rate, and past a limit the export fails.

Final Cut Pro export

Export an FCPXML with zoom keyframes, the camera as picture-in-picture (see Not there yet), and optional captions.

FCP Buffer

1 to 20 numbered clipboard slots (10 by default), each with one paste hotkey and a malibuclip://buffer/... URL you can call from elsewhere. Hotkeys are empty by default, and you fill slots from the menu, the panel or the URL.

Good to know

What you need, and what it does not do yet.

Requirements

  • macOS 26.0 or later
  • Apple Silicon Mac
  • Free trial (starts once you enter your email address), then a one-time license

Not there yet

  • Recording is still in beta: the whole Recording tab is labelled Recording (Beta) in the app
  • Studio Mode records without the cursor and draws it smoothed at export. The cursor is always an arrow there, and it is absent in the Final Cut Pro export
  • FCP Buffer slots are stored unencrypted
  • The recording features are new in 2.3 and 2.4 and have so far been verified by automated tests, not by long-term use
  • Known bug: a 16:9 camera image can appear distorted in the picture-in-picture
  • The Look (background, corners, highlight) applies to video and GIF export only, not to the Final Cut Pro export, and Look changes have no undo
  • No trim, no motion blur, no keystroke display and no cloud upload
