What's inside

What the current version (1.29) actually does.

Measure

Real-time EBU R128 loudness

Momentary, short-term and integrated LUFS plus loudness range (LRA), live, in a standalone app that needs no DAW. Since 1.29 the LUFS read-outs are only computed while the LUFS meter is visible, which keeps CPU use down.

Native multichannel

Natively measures 5-, 6- and 8-channel sources (5.0, 5.1, 7.1) with a fixed channel assignment (L R C LFE Ls Rs Lrs Rrs); other channel counts are downmixed to stereo. No Atmos or 7.1.4. 5.0 and 5.1 results are checked against EBU Tech 3341. Level bars and ballistics show the stereo downmix; LFE does not count in the bars.

Tap an app's audio

Four capture backends: AVAudioEngine input, AUHAL device, ScreenCaptureKit and Process Tap, used by the modes Device, App (Process Tap, macOS 14.4+) and Screen Audio. Process Tap compensates the mixdown attenuation automatically (heuristic).

Peak, True Peak, RMS, spectrum

Peak and RMS bars, True Peak (dBTP) with preset limits, a spectrum display and a Lissajous goniometer.

Five ballistics

Digital peak, PPM (EBU, DIN, Nordic) and VU. Scales: Linear, Final Cut Pro, DIN, VU, K-20/14/12 and EBU R128.

Test signal

Device mode only: Settings > Level Meter > Test Signal: Preview runs the meter on a mono sweep (100–8000 Hz) plus noise, not calibrated. Calibrated 997 Hz tones exist only as a 30 s WAV export (Settings > Input) to load into your NLE or Final Cut Pro.

Broadcast and streaming presets

EBU R128, Netflix, Apple TV+, YouTube, Spotify, Apple Music and ATSC A/85.

Your own status colours

Set the colours for too quiet, in range and too loud. Import and export settings and gradients.

Good to know

What you need, and what it does not do yet.

Requirements

  • macOS 14.0 or later; App mode (Process Tap) needs macOS 14.4, Device and Screen Audio run on 14.0+
  • 30-day trial (email required at first launch), then a license key unlocks the app
  • Permissions: Microphone (Device), Audio Capture (App), Screen Recording (Screen Audio)

Also good to know

  • The LUFS meter is off by default; switch it on in Settings > Spectrum
  • Live measurement only; there is no file analysis
  • Auto-reset on silence, always-on-top window and automatic updates via Sparkle
