Three steps

From your timeline to someone else's screen.

01

Send out of Final Cut Pro

Final Cut Pro's A/V Output goes to the virtual camera “Malibu Live Bridge”. The sound is captured from Final Cut Pro.

02

Share a code

Viewers join with a 6-character code. No account is needed to join.

03

They watch and talk

Your guests see and hear the cut and speak with you, on a Mac, iPad or iPhone.

What's inside

What the current beta (0.1.29) does.

Picture and sound

What you see in Final Cut Pro, they see too

Picture and sound from Final Cut Pro are sent to your viewers. It has been running in real test sessions, with viewers on iPad and iPhone hearing the editor.

Join with a code

A 6-character code is the invitation. Nobody has to create an account to watch.

Mac, iPad and iPhone viewers

Viewers run on Mac, iPad (iPadOS 17.5+) and iPhone (iOS 17.5+).

Watch and talk

Viewers see, hear and speak. That is the whole job, on purpose.

Your timeline stays yours

No markers, no scrubbing, no control over your timeline for viewers.

Direct or via server

Devices connect directly or through Studio Malibu's own servers.

Good to know

What you need, and what it does not do.

Requirements

  • Host: a Mac with Apple Silicon, macOS 15.0 or later
  • Final Cut Pro on the host
  • The camera extension “Malibu Live Bridge” has to be installed; a restart is needed
  • Viewers: Mac, or iPad and iPhone with iOS/iPadOS 17.5 or later
  • Currently in beta with testers (v0.1.29), not released yet, no date

What it does not do

  • No markers, no scrubbing and no control over the timeline. Viewers only see, hear and speak
  • No Apple TV, Windows, Android or web viewer
  • Not on the Mac App Store; distributed as a notarized download
  • Direct and relay connections are end-to-end encrypted. Connections via the server use transport encryption only
  • Planned price: €49. No release date yet
