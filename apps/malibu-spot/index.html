Four steps

From a cue in your cut to the song you hand over.

01

Drop in the picture

Put your cut's picture into Malibu Spot and set up a cue where music should come in.

02

Try songs on the cue

Drop local audio files onto the cue, or paste a Music or Spotify link, and hear each one against the picture.

03

Compare side by side

Play two candidates at the same Q-point with matched loudness, and decide by ear.

04

Hand over the winner

Send the music cues to Final Cut Pro.

What's inside

What Malibu Spot does today, as tested internally.

Audition

Local audio, against your picture

Audition audio files from your disk against the picture. Set an editable Q-point, the place in the song where the music enters, and add fades. Standard transport is built in: JKL, skim, snap and markers.

A/B at the same Q-point

Compare two candidates at the same Q-point, with BS.1770 loudness matching, so the louder song does not win by default.

Q-points from the beat

Get Q-point suggestions based on the beat, and snap your Q-point to the beat.

Add songs quickly

Drop a file on the picture, a cue or a song. Paste a Music or Spotify link. Attach a promo file.

Recognition aids

Thumbnails of the sync frame, colour labels per cue, and a “heard” history with an “unheard only” filter.

Cut versioning

Carry your cues over to a new cut by same timecode, by offset, or by audio match.

Hand over to Final Cut Pro

Send the music cues to Final Cut Pro.

Streaming candidates, as a preview

Links from Apple Music and Spotify give a preview against the picture, as well as they can. This is not working end to end yet.

Good to know

What you need, and what it does not do.

Status and requirements

  • Mac only, one window. macOS 14 or later
  • Works offline. No account or server needed; cues are stored locally, and syncing to a project server is optional
  • Tested internally only, not yet proven in production
  • Planned price: €19. No release date yet
  • The name “Malibu Spot” may still change

What it does not do

  • No web review link, no mobile version, no guest voting
  • The handover to Final Cut Pro does not handle start timecode yet
  • No Premiere or Avid export
  • Streaming audio has no real waveform and no exact beat grid
  • Apple Music and Spotify search is not available; streaming candidates are a preview that does not work end to end yet
