Three steps

From a card dump to a timeline-ready import.

01

Add your folders

Drop the video folder and the audio folder. Subfolders are read recursively, Add Raw Files covers loose material.

02

Match and check

Timecode overlap first, clap detection as fallback. Sync Check plays any clip with a frame-accurate timecode HUD, so you can nudge a sync by eye.

03

Export to Final Cut Pro

A finished FCPXML with synced clips, multicam, metadata and audio roles. Your media is never modified.

What's inside

What the current version (0.17) actually does.

Match

Timecode matching

Reads timecode from the MOV container and from BWF files. Drop-frame and non-drop-frame, common frame rates, and offspeed material with native slow-motion and real-time handling.

Sync Check

Built-in player with a frame-accurate timecode HUD, a jump to the clap point and slip nudging, so a sync is verified before it leaves the app.

Automatic channel selection

Analyzes every channel of the recorder file and picks the best one per clip, with per-channel role names read from iXML.

Smart Audio

Background analysis sets Voice Isolation, Loudness and Uniformity per clip.

Wild tracks

Wild tracks incl. an Atmos category, sorted the way you read them.

Multicam

Cameras with timecode overlap become multicam clips, or force them together.

Camera reports and Lockit

ALE and CSV reports plus Lockit metadata write Scene, Take, Reel and notes straight into Final Cut Pro's fields.

Trim modes and locked-only export

Trim to common, to video, or not at all, with audio pull-out. Export only the clips you locked.

Good to know

What you need, and what it does not do yet.

Requirements

  • macOS 14 or later, Final Cut Pro
  • Runs entirely on your Mac, nothing is uploaded
  • 10-day trial, then a one-time license

Not there yet

  • MOV, MP4, WAV and BWF are what we test with daily; MXF, R3D and BRAW material is not verified yet
  • Sync is timecode plus audio clap; waveform matching is planned, not built
