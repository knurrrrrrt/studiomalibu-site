The Workflow

Four steps. Director watches live.

🎬

Editor opens FCP + MalibuReview

MalibuReview activates the CMIO Extension as a virtual camera directly in FCP.

📡

Share session code

6-digit code. Director opens MalibuReview on iPhone, iPad, or Apple TV and connects.

🎥

Live stream starts

H.264 hardware-encoded over LiveKit WebRTC. Latency under 50 ms. Director sees exactly what the editor plays.

📌

Markers in real time

Reviewers set frame-accurate markers at the critical frame. Markers appear instantly at the editor. Export as FCPXML, EDL, CSV, or PDF.

What MalibuReview can do

What screen-share solutions can't do.

Frame.io, Vimeo Review, Wipster — all upload-based. MalibuReview streams live from Final Cut Pro, without a single file leaving the production infrastructure.

⚡

Direct FCP A/V output

The CMIO Extension taps FCP directly — hardware-encoded H.264, no screen capture. The director sees exactly what's in the FCP output, in broadcast quality.

⏱️

Frame-accurate SMPTE markers

Markers are tied to real SMPTE timecodes — including drop-frame (29.97DF). Not frame indexes, not timestamps. When exported as FCPXML they land pixel-accurately on the frame.

🔒

Zero-Trust-Verschlüsselung

AES-256-CTR, in-memory decryption — footage never lands unencrypted on any storage. For productions with NDAs, screener approvals, and piracy prevention.

📺

Native Apple TV app

The director sits at the living room TV or screening room and watches the live stream on Apple TV — with biometric proxy auth via iPhone. No laptop, no meeting link.

🔀

Gang mode

Multiple videos side by side with a synchronized playhead — for A/B comparisons, grading reviews, alternate cuts. Frame-accurate sync, unlimited streams.

🎵

Music supervision

Built-in mini-DAW for music supervision: audition candidates directly against the live edit stream, set Q-point markers, export cue sheets — without leaving the app.

🎙️

iPhone as a marker remote

Set voice markers straight from an iPhone — on-device speech recognition turns spoken notes into text markers, no tablet or keyboard required for directors.

✍️

Draw with Apple Pencil

Draw directly on the live picture — for framing notes, compositing comments, or VFX callouts, with full Apple Pencil support on iPad.

🛡️

Screener protection

Screen-recording blackout prevents capture, forensic watermarks make every session traceable, and access expires automatically — built for festival screeners and awards reviews.

⌨️

JKL shuttle & frame stepping

Full professional transport control — JKL shuttle, single-frame stepping, and slow-motion scrub — the same muscle memory editors already use in Final Cut Pro, now for reviewers too.

🔁

A/B loop review

Set in and out points and loop a section on repeat — for scrutinizing a single cut, transition, or VFX shot frame by frame.

📊

Real-time audio meters

A live, repositionable audio meter sidebar shows true peak levels as the stream plays — catch clipping, silence, or sync issues before they reach the mix.

Why MalibuReview

No upload. No screen share. Just FCP.

🎬 Direct from FCP

Other review tools require an export or a screen-share link. MalibuReview reads the FCP A/V output directly — no intermediate step, no waiting.

🔒 No file leaves production

What runs in the editor's FCP output is encrypted and ephemeral. No cloud server stores footage. Ideal for screener reviews, NDA productions, unreleased cuts.

⏱️ Markers land in FCP

After the session: FCPXML export, import into FCP once — and all markers are frame-accurate on the clips. No manual transfer, no lists to type.

📺 Apple TV instead of browser

Directors watch on the big screen in the living room or screening room. No video call platform, no shared window — just the MalibuReview tvOS app.

📤 Exports wherever you need

FCPXML for a direct reimport, EDL and CSV for Premiere workflows, PDF for note distribution, plus Frame.io export for teams that work there.
