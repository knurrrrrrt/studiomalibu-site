Your receiver.

Malibu Marantz is a native iOS remote control app for Marantz and compatible Denon AV receivers. It talks to your receiver directly over its own IP control protocol — the same protocol lineage used across most Marantz/Denon models of the last 15 years — for volume, inputs, surround modes, presets, zones, and your music library.

The Workflow

Enter the IP once. Control it every time.

📶

Enter the IP

Type in your receiver's IP address once — no discovery screen, no pairing, no account.

🔊

Control volume & inputs

Adjust volume across the receiver's real dB-calibrated range, and switch between TV, media player, game console, Blu-ray, phono, and more.

🎚️

Switch modes, recall presets

Jump between surround modes like Direct and Pure Direct, or recall a saved input, volume, and mode combination with one tap.

🎵

Browse & control zones

Browse your receiver's music library, pick an AirPlay route, and control additional zones — all from your phone.
