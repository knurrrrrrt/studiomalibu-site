The Workflow

Sign in once, control every lamp directly.

🔑

Sign in once

Use your existing Casambi or Occhio account credentials — the app joins your lamps' Casambi Bluetooth mesh from then on.

👥

Group your lamps

Combine multiple lamps — Kitchen counter, Dining table — into a group you control as one unit.

👆

Control with gestures

Drag horizontally for brightness, drag vertically to tilt the light direction, tap for on/off, double-tap to expand a group into its individual lamps.

🎬

Recall a scene

Dinner, Cooking, or any scene you've set up — tap the chip at the top of the screen.

Why Malibu Occhio

Built directly on the Bluetooth mesh your lamps already use.

⚡ Direct, not cloud-routed

The app talks straight to the Casambi Bluetooth mesh from your phone — no hub, no cloud round-trip needed just to turn a lamp on or off.

🔧 Classic & Evolution firmware

Works with both Casambi Classic and Evolution firmware — the BLE handshake is protocol-agnostic across both generations.

🔍 Independently verified protocol

The core Bluetooth protocol implementation is a reverse-engineered, independent Swift package — its cryptography was checked byte-for-byte against an open-source reference implementation. It is not an official Casambi or Occhio SDK.
