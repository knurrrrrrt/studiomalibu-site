Calls with camera bubbles,
chat and screen sharing.

Malibu Around lives in your menu bar. Each person in a call appears as a floating camera bubble on your screen, with a chat attached to it. You can send files, share your screen and talk, while you keep working.

Download for Mac
Always the latest beta build · macOS 15 or later · Apple Silicon only

Your login comes from the person who invited you. Without one, you can still join a single stream with a guest code.

Good to know

What the current beta does, and what it does not.

What it does

  • Video calls with floating camera bubbles.
  • Chat per bubble, and files by copying them in Finder (Cmd-C) and pasting into the chat (Cmd-V).
  • Screen sharing.
  • Joining a single stream without an account, with a guest code of 6 characters.
  • Updates arrive inside the app.

What it does not do

  • It does not run on Intel Macs or on macOS before 15.
  • You cannot create an account yourself. Accounts are created by the person who invited you.
  • You cannot set up your own users, teams or server. Everything runs on Studio Malibu's server for now.
  • With a guest code you see only that one stream and its chat.
  • It is a beta. Things can change or break between builds.

Getting started

  • Open the downloaded file and drag Malibu Around into your Applications folder.
  • Start it. It appears as an icon in the menu bar.
  • Sign in with the login you received, or choose the guest code option and enter your code.
  • Allow camera and microphone when macOS asks, and screen recording the first time you share your screen.
