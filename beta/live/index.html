Your cut, live
for whoever needs to watch.

Malibu Live sends Final Cut Pro's picture and sound to a director, client or producer on another Mac, iPad or iPhone. They watch and talk to you. They get no control over your timeline.

Download for Mac
Always the latest beta build · host: macOS 15 or later, Apple Silicon only

Viewers join with a code of 6 characters. No account is needed to watch.

Good to know

What you need, and what it does not do.

Requirements

  • Host: a Mac with Apple Silicon, macOS 15.0 or later
  • Final Cut Pro on the host
  • The camera extension “Malibu Live Bridge” has to be installed; a restart is needed
  • Viewers: Mac, or iPad and iPhone with iOS/iPadOS 17.5 or later

What it does not do

  • No markers, no scrubbing and no control over the timeline. Viewers only see, hear and speak
  • No Apple TV, Windows, Android or web viewer
  • Not on the Mac App Store; distributed as a notarized download
  • It is a beta. Things can change or break between builds
