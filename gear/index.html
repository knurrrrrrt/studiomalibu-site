FCP Workflow Tools

SyncScript ProStudio Malibu

Line-by-line keywording for Final Cut Pro — transcribes and matches every clip against the screenplay.

MalibuSyncStudio Malibu

Dual-system audio sync for Final Cut Pro — timecode matching, best-channel selection, ready FCPXML.

Sync-N-Link X

Batch-syncs picture to sound by timecode or waveform straight from an FCPXML.

Malibu BurnStudio MalibuSoon

Frame-accurate metadata burn-in for Final Cut Pro — timecode, clip name, reel, and camera, overlaid live.

Malibu ConformStudio MalibuSoon

Conforms Final Cut Pro timelines into DaVinci Resolve Studio — spatial/PAR-aware, multicam angle selection.

X2 Pro AAF Export

Exports FCP timelines to AAF for Pro Tools handoff.

EDL-X

Creates CMX 3600 EDLs from FCP XML for handoff to DaVinci Resolve, Smoke, and other post systems.

Merges script supervisor notes (CSV/ScriptE/Movie Slate) with FCP or Premiere clips.

Producers Best Friend

Formatted .xlsx report of clips, titles, and filters used across Events and Projects.

Lockit Network

Handles daily reports and metadata workflow from set to post.
