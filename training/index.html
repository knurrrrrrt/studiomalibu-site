Ripple Training

Steve Martin has been teaching Final Cut Pro since 1999. Core Training, Media Management, Sound, Color and Multicam courses, kept current with every FCP release — the closest thing to a structured curriculum for the app.

Jenn Jager

Two decades as a producer/editor, with weekly free FCP & Motion tutorials on YouTube and a structured paid course, "Final Cut Rock Star," updated for FCP 12 — she explains the why, not just the click-path.

Dylan Bates — The Final Cut Bro

Not a Final Cut Pro course itself — his paid Apple Motion Masterclass (41 lessons) teaches Apple Motion, the companion app, so you can build your own publishable Final Cut Pro templates and plugins. His regular free tutorials are proper FCP tips and live on our Videos page.

A word of caution

Not recommended

Pixel Film Studios

We wouldn't rely on Pixel Film Studios as an effects/plugin vendor. Independent reviews on Trustpilot and ComplaintsBoard, plus a long-running Apple Community thread, describe a pattern of purchased plugins not downloading, unresponsive or chatbot-only support, and rapid, low-quality-control releases.

This is one vendor's plugin store, not a training resource — it's flagged here because it keeps coming up in the same conversations as the trainers above.
