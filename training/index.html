A word of caution

Not recommended

Pixel Film Studios

We wouldn't rely on Pixel Film Studios as an effects/plugin vendor. Independent reviews on Trustpilot and ComplaintsBoard, plus a long-running Apple Community thread, describe a pattern of purchased plugins not downloading, unresponsive or chatbot-only support, and rapid, low-quality-control releases.

This is one vendor's plugin store, not a training resource — it's flagged here because it keeps coming up in the same conversations as the trainers above.
